Example 1 · everyday

Your power bill is usually $80, give or take $10. A $140 bill is z = (140 − 80) ÷ 10 = 6: call the utility.

z = (x − mean) ÷ s: how many standard deviations a value sits from the recent average. The dashed lines at plus and minus 3 are the trigger. Most dots cluster near zero, as expected. Clipping the 14.3 outlier at the top keeps the other 62 readable; the label says so, which is the honest way to clip a chart.
